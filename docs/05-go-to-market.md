# 05 · Go-to-market and launch campaign

## 1. Positioning

- **Category**: AI SDR for LinkedIn.
- **One-liner**: Hire an AI employee that prospects on LinkedIn, starts conversations, and books
  meetings into your calendar. You show up and close.
- **Who**: B2B sales teams of 2 to 20 reps, and solo sellers whose buyers live on LinkedIn
  (advisors, recruiters, consultants, agency owners, SaaS founders).
- **Against CoPilot AI**: same four-agent workflow, plus visible intent signals on every lead,
  an approval inbox so you trust it before you automate it, and a plan that works without
  Sales Navigator.
- **Against manual prospecting**: reps spend about 28% of their time selling (Salesforce, State of
  Sales). We take the other 72%.

## 2. Landing page (mirrors the CoPilot AI structure, our words and our numbers)

1. **Hero**: "Your AI SDR for LinkedIn." Sub: "Finds your buyers, starts the conversation, books
   the meeting. You close." CTA: Start 7-day free trial. Secondary: Watch a 2-minute demo.
2. **Proof strip**: six stats, filled from design-partner results (placeholders until week 10).
3. **How it works**: Strategy Agent, Targeting Agent, Reply Agent, You close. Each with two bullets
   and a short product GIF, CTA repeated.
4. **Did you know?** the Salesforce 28% stat with source.
5. **Built for teams**: team management, CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier, webhooks),
   real humans on support.
6. **See the signals**: a screenshot of a lead card with intent signals, our clearest differentiator.
7. **Testimonials**: named partner quotes with title and company, photos where permitted.
8. **Pricing**: three plans, trial on all.
9. **FAQ**: Is it safe for my LinkedIn account? Do I need Sales Navigator? Can I approve every
   message? What happens when a prospect asks a hard question? How does it connect to my CRM?
10. **Trial form** anchored, every CTA scrolls here. Fields: name, work email, company, LinkedIn
    URL, team size.

## 3. Launch campaign (weeks 9 to 14)

| Channel | Play | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-led LinkedIn content | Dogfood the product publicly. Weekly "meetings booked this week" post with real screenshots; behind-the-scenes on how the Reply Agent decides to hand off. 3 posts per week from 2 founders. | 50k impressions per week by launch |
| Outbound (our own product) | Run LinkedIn Employee on the ICP of sales leaders at 5 to 50 person B2B companies. This is the campaign and the case study at once. | 40 demos booked pre-launch |
| Cold email | Reuse the cold email server for a sequence to VP Sales and founders, pointing to the demo video. | 2% reply, 0.5% demo |
| Comparison and SEO pages | "LinkedIn Employee vs CoPilot AI", "vs Dripify", "vs Expandi", "AI SDR for LinkedIn", "book meetings on LinkedIn automatically". | Rank top 10 within 90 days |
| Launch day | Product Hunt, LinkedIn launch post with a 90-second demo, partner posts from the 10 design partners the same morning, email to the waitlist. | 500 trial starts in launch week |
| Review sites | G2 and Capterra listings seeded by partners. | 20 reviews in 60 days |
| Partners | Agency and consultant referral program at 20% recurring for 12 months. | 5 partners at launch |
| Paid (after organic data) | LinkedIn Ads to sales titles retargeting site visitors; Google Ads on competitor and category terms. | CAC under 3 months of revenue |

## 4. Onboarding funnel (built into the product)

1. Trial start (card, 7 days).
2. Connect LinkedIn + calendar (day 0), Strategy Agent runs during signup so the first screen is
   their Business Profile, not an empty dashboard.
3. Strategy call auto-offered on day 1, review call on day 5.
4. First campaign launched by day 1, first replies by day 3, first meeting target by day 10.
5. Day 6 email: what the agent did this week, with the upgrade prompt.

Trial-to-paid target: 20% in month one, 30% by month three.

## 5. Content backlog for launch

- 2-minute product demo video, one 20-second clip per agent.
- "Is LinkedIn automation safe?" guide (our caps and pause logic, honestly explained).
- ICP worksheet generated by the Strategy Agent as a free lead magnet (no login, email gate).
- Three case studies from design partners in the CoPilot AI testimonial format:
  situation, what the agent did, numbers, quote.
- Comparison pages listed above.

## 6. What we measure weekly

Trial starts, activation (LinkedIn connected + campaign launched within 24 hours), meetings booked
per seat, trial-to-paid, churn, restriction incidents, CAC by channel, and the proof-strip stats.
